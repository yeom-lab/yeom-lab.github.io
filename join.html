Join the Lab

If you are interested in joining the lab, please send an e-mail to Jinki Yeom (jinki.yeom@snu.ac.kr) with:

Undergraduate students

  • A short message about your scientific interests
  • Your CV
  • Your commitment to lab work (full-time or part-time)

You can also apply through the summer or winter internship program of the Department of Biomedical Sciences at SNU.

Graduate students

  • A short message about your scientific interests
  • Your CV
  • Your undergraduate transcript

Graduate students are supported by the BK21 program.

Postdoctoral researchers

  • A cover letter describing your scientific interests (e.g., microbiology, biochemistry, immunology or bioinformatics)
  • Your CV
  • Contact information for at least two references
